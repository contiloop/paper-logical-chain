# Rules for a logical chain

Read by the author and by every reviewer. The layout of the chain is in `prompts/formats/logical-chain.md`.

## What a chain is

A logical chain is the argument of one research paper, told to a researcher of the same field who has not read the paper: the problem, the idea, why it should work, what was found, and what follows. The person who asked for it will build a talk from it without re-reading the paper.

The chain speaks for the paper. It tells the argument as the paper makes it, and it does not report on the paper: how the paper is written, what it cites, what it leaves undone and where it could be faulted are no part of the talk.

The paper is in the workspace as `source/<input>/`, with an anchor on every paragraph. So the chain carries the line of the argument and links the rest: a reader who wants a derivation, a setting or a table follows the link.

## Three things that must hold

1. **A listener can follow it.** Each claim comes with what makes it follow from what came before, in a clause or a sentence, and a link to where the paper explains it. A person who read earlier chains was stopped where a result came with no reason, where a general claim had no instance, where two statements could not both be held, and where the thing discussed changed with no word on how the two relate.
2. **It claims what the paper claims, and no more.** An assumption stays an assumption, and a result of one experiment stays that; the limits the paper itself puts on a claim stay with the claim. What a step says of a figure or a table is true of it: look at it first.
3. **It holds what the talk needs, and nothing else.** The author first writes the spine: the paper's conclusion, and under it, in order, the claims that conclusion rests on. A step is a link of the spine. Whatever can be removed with the way to the conclusion still standing, and the talk no different, is removed.

These are judged as the person giving the talk would judge them, not one sentence at a time against a list.

## Language

The chain is in the language the person asked for, and in English when they asked for none, written as a writer of that language writes.
